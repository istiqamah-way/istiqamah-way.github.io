Istiqamah Studio · Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Istiqamah Studio is a private tool used only by the Istiqamah Way channel to publish its own content. It has no other users and collects no data from viewers.

What it stores

When the channel owner connects TikTok (or YouTube, Instagram), the sign-in tokens and basic profile information (account id, display name, avatar) are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder that is never shared, uploaded or published.

What it uses them for

Only to upload the channel's own approved videos to the channel's own account when the owner asks for it, and to show which account is connected.

What it does not do

It does not read, collect, sell or share anyone's personal data, messages, followers or viewing activity, and it shows no ads.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect at any time in the studio (Connections) or in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Apps), and delete the local folder.
